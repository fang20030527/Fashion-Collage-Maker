# Fashion Collage Preview Assets

The PNG files in `public/fashion-collage/examples/` and `public/fashion-collage/templates/` are self-made, programmatically generated local bitmap assets for the Fashion Collage Maker MVP.

- No external source images or hotlinked third-party assets are used at runtime.
- No real brand logos, trademarks, celebrity likenesses, or influencer photos are included.
- Human-fashion content is limited to abstract mannequins, garments, fabric/detail scenes, and travel-fashion still-life placeholders.
- Template thumbnails are intended to preview the MVP export layouts for Editorial Hero, Clean Grid, Overlapped Print, and Lookbook Strip.
- These assets are local project files created for MVP examples and template previews.
